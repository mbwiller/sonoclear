# Imaging sources

These mammogram views are de-identified images from the Digital Mammography Dataset for
Breast Cancer Diagnosis Research (DMID), PNG conversion, used here for demonstration and
educational purposes only. They stand in for the fictional patient's images in this preview.

Source: DMID, Digital Mammography Dataset for Breast Cancer Diagnosis Research (Oza, Sharma, Patel et al.), supplied by the founder for this demo. Confirm the dataset license before any public release.
